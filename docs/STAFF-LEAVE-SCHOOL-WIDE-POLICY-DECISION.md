# School-wide leave policy — owner decision

Status: school-wide policy intent recorded; legal applicability and exact day-counting details remain a release blocker pending review against the school's approved source and each applicable employment instrument.

## Decision from school owner
The school owner has explicitly confirmed that **all personnel at Wat Bueng Nam Sai School use the same leave rules**. Do not create separate leave entitlements or different day-counting rules by personnel category in the application.

## Implementation requirements
- Maintain one school-wide leave policy catalogue, with one authoritative version effective for the relevant dates.
- All personnel use the same approved leave types, eligibility, day-counting basis, weekend/holiday treatment, partial-day handling, cancellation rules, and approval workflow, as specified in that policy.
- Personnel category may remain as profile/reporting metadata. It must not silently create different school-policy rules; however, the application must never reduce a mandatory statutory, regulatory, contract, or collective-agreement minimum that applies to an individual. Any unavoidable legal floor must be represented transparently and documented, not hidden as a personnel-category preference.
- The policy must still cite the actual official regulation/order/manual and record the approved effective date and version. The owner's statement establishes the intended school-wide application, but does not itself establish specific entitlement numbers or override any legally binding rule.
- If the source materials do not support a specific day limit or counting detail, mark it for authorized school confirmation instead of guessing.
- A school-wide administrative rule is not a waiver of mandatory legal rights. Before enabling calculations, the school must confirm which legal regime applies to each engagement type and whether a single rule can lawfully satisfy all staff.

## Legal source check — childbirth assistance (reviewed 2026-11-03)
- The Government Public Relations Department summary states that the Labour Protection Act (No. 9) B.E. 2568 took effect on 7 December 2025; it describes 15 days of paid spouse/partner childbirth assistance leave, usable before or within 90 days after childbirth, for employees covered by that Act. It also describes protections for outsourced service workers in government agencies. Source: https://secretary.prd.go.th/th/content/category/detail/id/9/iid/451880
- The Office of the Civil Service Commission (OCSC) FAQ for government employees states a 90-day filing window and up to 15 consecutive working days, while payment after day 30 is treated separately under the applicable rules. Source: https://www.ocsc.go.th/faq/government-or-permanent-employee/ge-benefits/
- These are not interchangeable regimes. The 30-day condition must not be used as a universal filing deadline, and the 90-day rule must not be generalized to every staff engagement without confirming the governing instrument. The school-provided handbook and the school's employment/appointment instruments must be reconciled by an authorized officer before policy activation.
- Dashboard reports should use the same shared rule set for all school personnel and may still filter statistics by personnel category for analysis, without applying different policy rules.

## Required test coverage
- Verify that equivalent leave requests use the same school-wide baseline policy and calendar; separately verify that any legally mandatory minimum applicable to the individual is never reduced.
- Verify that category changes do not rewrite previously submitted or approved leave requests.
- Verify that every request stores the shared policy version used at submission/decision time.
- Verify that missing/unverified rules or unresolved legal applicability block unsupported automatic calculations.
