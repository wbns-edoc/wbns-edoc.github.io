# Review of the supplied leave handbook — evidence and policy gates

Reviewed: 2026-10-22  
Source: user-provided 33-page scanned PDF, “คู่มือการปฏิบัติงานเกี่ยวกับการลาประเภทต่าง ๆ ของข้าราชการ”, attributed to the Office of the Permanent Secretary, Ministry of Education's ICT Center. The PDF metadata indicates creation in 2024, but the handbook itself explicitly cites the Prime Minister's Regulation on Civil Service Leave B.E. 2555 (2012).

## What was actually extracted

The file is image-based (no usable embedded text layer); OCR was used to inspect its cover, introduction, contents, definitions, leave-process diagram, and selected leave-type pages. OCR can misread Thai numerals and must not be treated as a legally authoritative transcription.

The handbook identifies 11 categories of leave, including sick leave, maternity leave, leave to assist a wife after childbirth, personal leave, annual vacation, ordination/Hajj, military call-up/training, study/training/research/observation, international-organization work, accompanying a spouse, and occupational rehabilitation.

Selected values visible in the scanned pages include:
- Sick leave: 20 working days per year in the summary, with an additional allowance of up to 20 working days in necessary cases; medical-certificate requirements are described on the detailed page.
- Annual vacation: 10 working days per fiscal year, with carry-over limits of 20 or 30 working days depending on years of service.
- Leave to assist a wife after childbirth: 15 working days, subject to a filing window stated in the handbook.
- The maternity-leave page's OCR reads 50 days, which is a clear warning that this handbook is not safe to use as the current policy source without checking later amendments.

These are recorded only as historical source observations, **not** as configured application entitlements.

## Mandatory policy verification before implementation

1. Do not encode the scanned handbook's figures as current law. It expressly relies on the 2012 civil-service leave regulation, and at least some figures appear outdated or need validation against later amendments.
2. Confirm the current rules for every leave type from current official Thai legal instruments and applicable personnel rules. A school-wide product setting can apply one approved rule set to all school personnel only after the school has formally confirmed that those same entitlements legally apply to civil servants, government employees, and contract teachers alike.
3. Preserve distinctions between calendar days and working days, fiscal year and calendar year, eligibility/tenure, carry-over, required evidence, notice windows, salary treatment, and exception authority.
4. Keep approval routing blocked when no authorized Vice Director is assigned. A Director's authority must be verified by leave type and duration; do not assume that a school Director can approve every category/length of leave or silently substitute a System Admin.
5. Cancellation cut-off (at least one day before the leave start) is a school workflow requirement and must be implemented separately from statutory entitlements, with any permitted exception explicitly authorized and audited.
6. Do not infer missing or garbled OCR values. If a legal rule cannot be confirmed from an official current source, mark that rule unconfigured and block release of that leave type.
